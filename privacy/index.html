Privacy policy

This privacy policy applies to the Tantos Domino Scorekeeper app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Levi Labs (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application keeps the games you play (team and player names, scores and settings) on your device.

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

Photos taken to count domino pips are analyzed on your device and are never uploaded.

Cloud backup (Tantos Pro)

Cloud backup is optional. It is only active if you have Tantos Pro and turn it on yourself by signing in with your Google account. Without it, your games stay only on your device.

When cloud backup is on, the Application stores:

  • Account information from your Google account: your email address, your name, your profile picture link, and a user ID assigned by Firebase.
  • Your finished matches: team and player names as you typed them, points scored in each hand, the house rules used, scores, the target score, and when each match started and ended.
  • Technical information: the platform (Android) and app version, and the date the account was created.

This data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) on Google Cloud servers in the United States. Google processes it on the Service Provider's behalf. It is encrypted in transit and at rest, and security rules allow only your own account to read or change your matches. The Service Provider does not sell this data or use it for advertising.

To protect the service from misuse, the Application uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity, which checks that requests come from the genuine Application on a genuine device.

If you sign out, your matches stay on your device and in the cloud, but stop being backed up. If you lose Tantos Pro, syncing stops, and your cloud data is kept until you delete it.

Deleting your account and cloud data: in the Application, go to Settings › Cloud backup › Delete cloud data. Your account and all your matches in the cloud are deleted immediately; matches on your device are kept. You can also request deletion without the Application at https://app-tantos-android.web.app/delete-account, or by email at soporte.tantosapp+eliminar@gmail.com. Requests by email are completed within 30 days.

Data on your device: Android's automatic backup may copy your games to your own Google account. That backup is managed by Google, and you can turn it off in your device's settings.

Third Party Access

The Application uses the following third-party services, which process data on the Service Provider's behalf:

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, to understand how the Application is used and to fix crashes. They receive usage events, device information and app instance identifiers.
  • Google AdMob, to show ads in the free version. It may use your device's advertising ID. Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the Application asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Google Play Billing, to process the Tantos Pro purchase. The Service Provider does not receive your payment details.
  • Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and App Check, only if you turn on Cloud backup (see above).

The Service Provider may share your information with third parties in the ways that are described in this privacy statement.

The Service Provider may disclose User Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with their trusted services providers who work on their behalf, do not have an independent use of the information we disclose to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network. Uninstalling does not delete data stored with Cloud backup: to delete it, use Settings › Cloud backup › Delete cloud data in the Application, or visit https://app-tantos-android.web.app/delete-account.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider will retain User Provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter. If you'd like them to delete User Provided Data that you have provided via the Application, please contact them at soporte.tantosapp+eliminar@gmail.com and they will respond in a reasonable time.

Feedback and the country survey

Tantos has optional forms to report a problem, suggest a feature, tell me the app isn't for you, and say how dominoes is played where you live. When you send one, I receive what you wrote and the options you checked, your country, the app's version and language, and that you use Android. A problem report also includes your phone model and Android version, so I can find the bug. If you leave your email, I use it only to reply to you.

These answers are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). They aren't linked to your name, your account or any device or advertising ID, and I don't sell or share them. I keep them for as long as they help me improve Tantos.

To have your feedback deleted, write to soporte.tantosapp@gmail.com. If you left your email in the form, I can find and delete everything you sent with it.
